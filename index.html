Who we are

Creating Opportunities.
Building Resilient Futures.

Kopano CyberPath NGO is a Lesotho‑based non‑profit committed to closing the digital skills gap. We focus on cybersecurity education, mentorship, and partnerships to create meaningful opportunities for orphaned and underserved youth.

  • Cybersecurity & digital literacy workshops
  • Mentorship from industry professionals
  • University & corporate collaborations
  • Safe, inclusive learning environments
Support our mission

Our Vision

Empower vulnerable youth in Lesotho with the knowledge, skills, and support they need to thrive in the digital world and become leaders in technology and cybersecurity.

Our Mission

To equip orphaned and underserved youth with digital & cybersecurity skills through education, mentorship, and strong partnerships.
