KOPANO CYBERPATH NGO is a Lesotho-based non- profit organisation established to open pathways into cybersecurity and technology careers for high school and college students across Lesotho and, through its growing partner network, the wider region. Many students with real aptitude and interest in ICT never get the chance to develop it, simply because their schools are not resourced to provide structured mentorship, specialised curriculum, or real-world exposure. At the same time, Lesotho currently has no formal, standardised cybersecurity training pathway for teachers, even as teachers are already expected to supervise learners' use of digital devices, handle student records, and model safe online behaviour. Kopano Cyberpath exists to close both gaps at once: identifying students and educators with potential in technology, and walking with them through mentorship, training, international cohorts, and hands-on opportunities until they are equipped to build, secure, and lead in the digital economy.

The Curriculum

Four phases. One path from first login to first job.

Delivered in person at orphanages and community centres, hybrid where connectivity allows, and via mobile labs in remote areas.

01

Digital Literacy & Basic Tech

Computers, internet, safe browsing, file management built on Khan Academy, Code.org, and Scratch.

→ Build confidence and foundational skills.
02

Cybersecurity 101

Phishing, passwords, malware, digital footprints, and privacy taught through simulations, games, and real-world scenarios using NIST and OWASP awareness modules.

→ Recognise and resist real threats.
03

Coding & Tech Skills

Python, HTML/CSS, and core logic in VS Code, GitHub, and Jupyter applied to personal websites, games, and mini apps.

→ Ship a first real project.
04

Career & Digital Citizenship

Resume writing, presentation skills, ethical tech, and mentorship with optional internships alongside university partners.

→ Walk out with a next step.

Core Programs

01

School Partnership & Mentorship Programme

Working with a founding group of students at partner schools such as our proposed partnership with Masitise High School Kopano facilitators deliver local teaching and mentorship, building foundational skills, discipline, and confidence in cybersecurity and technology.

02

International Cohort Progression

Students who engage well and show progress are invited to join online cohorts alongside peers from our partner countries Ghana, South Africa, and India broadening their skills and their network.

03

Cybersecurity Awareness Seminars for Educators

One-day seminars for pre-service and practising teachers such as our proposed seminar for Lesotho Teaching College covering digital footprint management, phishing and scams, social media professionalism, protecting student data, misinformation, and personal cybersecurity.

04

High-School-Based Hackathons

Once students have built up their skills, Kopano organises hackathons hosted at the high school level, giving students a real, hands-on setting to apply what they have learned and showcase their work.

05

Builder-to-Defender Partnership Programme

Alongside data, AI, and software-engineering training delivered by one of our partner organisations from another region in Africa, Kopano introduces cybersecurity masterclasses, secure-coding principles, and security-architecture sessions, helping learners understand how to protect and secure what they build.

06

Teacher Development & Digital Safety Training

Structured orientation sessions that prepare educators to guide learners toward responsible online choices while safeguarding learners' personal data and information.
