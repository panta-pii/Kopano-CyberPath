The Curriculum

Four phases. One path from first login to first job.

Delivered in person at orphanages and community centres, hybrid where connectivity allows, and via mobile labs in remote areas.

01

Digital Literacy & Basic Tech

Computers, internet, safe browsing, file management — built on Khan Academy, Code.org, and Scratch.

→ Build confidence and foundational skills.
02

Cybersecurity 101

Phishing, passwords, malware, digital footprints, and privacy — taught through simulations, games, and real-world scenarios using NIST and OWASP awareness modules.

→ Recognise and resist real threats.
03

Coding & Tech Skills

Python, HTML/CSS, and core logic in VS Code, GitHub, and Jupyter — applied to personal websites, games, and mini apps.

→ Ship a first real project.
04

Career & Digital Citizenship

Resume writing, presentation skills, ethical tech, and mentorship — with optional internships alongside university partners.

→ Walk out with a next step.

University Collaboration

We co-host, universities co-own.

Free workshops, mentorship, and co-developed "Tech for Youth" modules — built as a two-way partnership from the first pilot onward.

Hackathons for Heroes

Students team up to solve real-world problems for their communities.

Tech Tuesdays

Monthly sessions held in campus halls and public libraries.

Cyber Awareness Week

Campus-wide campaigns promoting digital safety.

Code for Change

Youth-led tech projects with measurable community impact.

What Universities Gain

  • Academic or co-curricular credit where possible
  • Guest speaking opportunities for staff and students
  • Joint research and capstone project support
  • Mentorship and internship pipelines

First Steps

  1. 01 Reach out to two universities for pilot workshops
  2. 02 Draft a simple, mutual partnership agreement
  3. 03 Host the first workshop with 10–20 students
Local Roots, Global Standard

A secure bridge between Lesotho's youth and the world's tech economy.

Every module we teach is measured against international frameworks — NIST and OWASP so a student trained in Maseru is trained to a standard recognised anywhere.
